      How to give

      1. Pick the network in the box above. TRON (TRC-20) usually has the lowest fees.
      2. Copy the address or scan the QR code with your wallet app.
      3. Send any amount of USDT. Check that the network in your wallet matches the network you picked.
      4. Optional: email us the transaction hash and we will send you a receipt.

      Questions

      What happens if I send on the wrong network?

      Funds sent on a network that does not match the address can be lost. Always match the network in your wallet to the one you selected here. If you are unsure, send a small test amount first.

      Can I donate other coins?

      No. We accept USDT only, so that every gift is stable in value and easy to account for.

      Is my donation tax deductible?

      Tax rules depend on your country and on our legal status. Ask us for a receipt and check with your tax adviser.

      Can I stay anonymous?

      Yes. Blockchain transactions show only wallet addresses. You do not need to tell us who you are.
